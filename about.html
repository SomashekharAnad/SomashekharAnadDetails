Profile

Automation and Industrial IoT engineer with 3+ years across ABB Edgenius IoT, Symphony Plus and 800xA. I configure, test and validate secure edge-to-cloud data flows over OPC UA, Modbus and PubSub, and have delivered Symphony Plus upgrades and commissioning at major NTPC and JSW power stations.
